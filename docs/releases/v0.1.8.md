## Summary

`parakeet-cli` v0.1.8 gives the last committed session segment the same 0.3 s silent run-out previews already use, and drops trailing analog room tone before that pad. Short takes and list-final words that 0.1.7 cut off (`zebra`, `dates`) now decode on the microphone path. Protocol 2 is unchanged.

## What's Changed

- `Segmenter::finish()` trims owned samples below an amplitude of 0.01 (MacBook rest is ~0.002 peak) and appends `PREVIEW_TAIL_SILENCE_SAMPLES` (0.3 s of digital zeros), owning those frames so TDT can emit the last word
- analog hangover after the last loud sample is not kept: it makes the decoder invent a tail (`"The final word is a very important thing."` for `"The final word is zebra"`)
- middle CORE segments are unchanged; they already have right context
- a sub-window stop still keeps its samples, plus the silent run-out
- collection drain and overrun accounting tests treat the run-out as padding, not recorded audio

## Integration Migration

No change is required. `complete` text is still the only committed transcript. Superkeet and other protocol-2 clients pick this up by bundling the v0.1.8 binary.

## Validation and Known Limitations

- 56 Rust tests pass, including sample conservation, stop draining, room-tone trim, and quiet-take preservation
- through-air Samantha replay: immediate-stop `"The final word is Zebra."`; list-from-five recovers `eight, dates`
- `auth`/`off` and `err`/`air` substitutions are unchanged model confusions
- quiet through-air (Fred at this Mac's default volume) can still fall below Silero
- protocol version remains 2; `parakeet --version` prints `0.1.8`
